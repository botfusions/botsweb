Every room opens onto the water.

Dinner arrives in nine courses, and in no hurry.

Chef Aoki Ren has cooked in this kitchen for twenty-two years. The old calendar has seventy-two seasons of five days each, and his menu follows every one of them.

The seasons at Still Water 四季

The same pond, four times a year.

  1. SpringMarch – April

    Cherry petals drift over the wall from the temple next door and gather in the corners of the pond. The koi wake up hungry.

  2. SummerJune – August

    The lotus open at dawn and close by noon. Fireflies over the water in the first week of July, and the veranda doors stay open all night.

  3. AutumnOctober – November

    The maple turns. Nine days when the pond is more red than green; most guests book them a year ahead.

  4. WinterDecember – February

    Snow on the lantern’s roof. The koi rest deep and slow under the ice-cold spring, and the house is at its most silent.
