01 Rev it

Wake her up.

A 1968 air-cooled parallel twin, bored to 790cc and rebuilt from the crank out. We re-phased it to a 270° firing order, so it lopes like a V-twin at idle and pulls clean to 8,500.

  • Hold mouse, space or the button to open the throttle.
  • Let go above five thousand for the crackle on the overrun.
  • Watch the headlight — on a 1968 charging system it brightens with the revs.

04 The garage

We open when
the city closes.

Nightshift started in 2011 as two dock mechanics working the hours nobody wanted — ten at night to six in the morning, in a borrowed bay under the Maas bridge. The hours stuck. So did the rain.

Fifteen years later there are four of us, two bays and a neon sign that has buzzed since the night we hung it. Nobody answers the phone before dark. Everybody answers it after.
