III  ·  The jewellery

Cut from the stones we kept.

When a specimen is too fractured to sell whole, our cutter in Idar-Oberstein takes what is left and sets it in platinum. Each piece is engraved inside with the number of the stone it came from.

IV  ·  Provenance & ethics

Every stone arrives
with its paperwork.

  1. 01

    Named, not sourced

    We buy from thirty-one miners and families we know by name — never at auction. Each label carries the GPS locality, the date it came out of the ground and who found it.

  2. 02

    Untreated, always

    No heat, no dye, no resin, no reassembly. If a specimen has ever been repaired, the label says so, in ink, on the front.

  3. 03

    We publish what we paid

    On average 41% of a sale price goes back to the mine. The trade norm is under five. The figure for every stone is printed on its certificate.
