Lanternholm

3 h from Port Aubade · the lighthouse isle

The only lighthouse in the world that has never seen the sea. Keeper Oona Brisk lights the lamp at dusk anyway — for airships, for swifts, and for anyone who has lost their bearings above the clouds.

  • Climb the 112 steps to the lamp room at dusk
  • Seed cake and strong tea in the keeper’s cottage
  • Lupin meadows in flower from May to August

Amberlea

4½ h from Port Aubade · the drifting farm

The breadbasket of the archipelago: wheat that grows in thin air, a mill that has not stopped turning since 1904, and a river that runs off the end of the farm into nothing at all.

  • Bring in the July harvest with the Amberlea families
  • Row the mill-river right to the edge of the world
  • A warm loaf from the mill with every return berth

Hollowmere

6 h from Port Aubade · the endless falls

A forest lake that never empties, pouring over the island’s lip into two thousand metres of air. Nobody has found where the water comes from. Everybody agrees it tastes of rain.

  • Swim the pool a stone’s throw from the edge
  • Cross the moss bridge to the Wayfinder Stones
  • Canopy hammocks, with rainbows at breakfast

Hanakumo

9 h from Port Aubade · overnight, by sleeper

A three-tiered temple whose cherry trees bloom every month the island drifts south. The monks ring the wind bells by hand when a ship comes in — you will hear Hanakumo an hour before you see it.

  • The dawn bell ceremony on the upper gallery
  • The lantern stair: through the torii, up 208 steps
  • Petal season — we will wake you when it starts
