L'Atelier · since 1962

Sixty years on rue du Bac.

Odile Marchand opened the shop in the spring of 1962 with one oven, a copper basin and a recipe she refused to write down. Her granddaughter Camille still whips the meringue in that basin.

What changed: the ovens, and the length of the queue on Saturday mornings. What didn't: the whites are aged for three days, the almonds are ground before sunrise, and every shell is piped by hand — by five people who could do it with their eyes closed, and occasionally do.
