    01 — Cold start

    Standby to standing weight in 9.2 seconds.

    The Warden idles at 0.3% reactor draw — just enough to keep the gyros warm and the coolant moving. What you just did is the fastest cold start of any frame in its class. Every pilot in the program has done it a few hundred times, and every one of them still grins when the lights come on.

    1. T+0.0Ignition. The salt core reaches operating temperature in 3.1 s. You feel it through the seat first.
    2. T+3.4Handshake. Forty-one actuators report in, heel to crown, in 0.6 s.
    3. T+9.2Sync. The harness calibrates to your reflexes — not the other way round.

    02.1 Systems · Reactor

    A 42-megawatt heart, sealed for life.

    A molten-salt core the size of a beer keg sits behind the chest plate. It is sealed at the factory and never opened again. It makes enough power for a small town and enough heat to boil the rain off the armour.

    Every joint is an electro-hydraulic actuator with its own controller and its own opinion. The knees carry 1,900 kilonewtons at full stride and still correct for a loose paving slab in under a millisecond. You decide where to go. They decide not to fall over.
