A full-length carbon plate, thirty-eight millimetres of nitrogen-set foam and an upper you can see daylight through. We built a lens so you can check our work.

01 The lens

Every shoe says it’s fast. Ours lets you look.

Outside the glass it’s a product shot. Inside, it’s the lab file: what the plate does, where the heat goes, and every dimension we argued about for fourteen months.

02 Plate

A spoon, not a spring.

1.1 mm of unidirectional carbon, laid in a scooped curve. It stiffens the forefoot so your toes stop spending energy on flexing, then rolls you forward off a 4.2° rocker. It doesn’t bounce. It just refuses to waste anything.

8mmHeel-to-toe drop
  • 1.1 mm plate thickness
  • 4.2° toe spring
  • 62% rocker apex

03 Foam

Thirty-eight millimetres of almost nothing.

VX Foam is a supercritical PEBA, expanded with nitrogen until it is 81% air. Under the thermal lens you can watch it take a footstrike, spread the load across the heel and hand it forward.

38mmHeel stack height
  • 87% energy return
  • 0.09 g/cm³ density
  • 30 mm forefoot

04 Upper

Built from one thread and an argument.

A single-layer engineered mesh, knitted in one piece with 212 zones of different density — open where your foot runs hot, closed where it needs holding. The ripstop cage over the midfoot weighs less than the laces.

186gPer shoe, US 9
  • 212 knit zones
  • 0 stitched seams
  • 11 g ripstop cage
