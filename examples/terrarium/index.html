Inside the glass

Five layers, one small water cycle.

Nothing in a sealed terrarium is decoration. Every layer has a job, and between them they turn a single glass of water into a decade of weather.

  1. 01

    Moss & planting

    Set by hand, one cushion at a time.

  2. 02

    Living soil

    Peat-free loam, bark, perlite and springtails.

  3. 03

    Fine mesh

    Keeps the soil out of the drainage.

  4. 04

    Activated charcoal

    Keeps the water sweet and the air clean.

  5. 05

    Drainage stones

    Where the rain waits until it’s needed.

  6. 06

    Walnut base

    One Herefordshire board. Oiled, never lacquered.

Care

Light: bright, indirect. Water: never.

07:00The soil warms and the glass fogs over.

  • Somewhere bright

    A north or east window is perfect. Summer sun turns a cloche into a greenhouse, so keep it a metre back.

  • Leave the bell on

    Condensation is the point, not a problem. If the glass stays fogged all day, lift it for an hour, once.

  • Trim in spring

    Moss is a lawn. Brass scissors come with every cloche, and so does a reminder, every March.

  • Call us in 2031

    Every bell is guaranteed for ten years. If yours ever needs water, we’ll come round and give it some.
