03 — The bar · B1, 2-chōme

Twelve seats. One rule you’ll actually keep.

We are a listening bar in the old kissa tradition: the records are the menu, the speakers are the view, and conversation happens between sides. Sit down, order something brown, and let a whole album go by.

House rule

No phones on the counter.

Put it in the drawer under your seat. You will get it back at the end of the side.

Hours

Wed — Sun
19:00 — 02:00

Last side goes on at 01:30. Closed Mondays, Tuesdays and whenever the owner is digging in Osaka.

Seats

12 at the counter

No standing, no tables, no groups over four. Six seats are held for walk-ins every night.

Requests

On paper, not out loud

Write it on the card by your glass. Both sides, always. We never skip a track.
